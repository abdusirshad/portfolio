Architecture Decision Case Study

TRecos: production RAG on AKS without adding search-stack sprawl.

For the recommendation platform, the important decision was keeping inference, retrieval, deployment, and observability close enough to operate as one production system while still leaving room for model iteration.

Challenge

Ship low-latency AI recommendations inside a regulated environment with GPU workloads, traceable prompts, repeatable releases, and minimal operational surface area.

Tradeoff

Used AKS, Azure OpenAI, and pgvector instead of a separate managed vector/search tier, reducing moving parts while keeping retrieval tunable through database-level controls.

Result

Sub-second recommendations, model-to-production cycles under three days, CI security gates, and platform observability through Langfuse, Azure Monitor, and Grafana.
